Four-Tier Clinical Decision Workflow

★ Tier 1 (>90% Score)

Exceptional synergy and verified immunological or RTK co-engagement. Top-priority candidates nominated directly for in vitro binding and cellular killing assays.

Tier 2 (75%–90% Score)

Strong biological compatibility. Solid candidates for translational lead optimization and tumor xenograft efficacy validation.

Tier 3 (60%–75% Score)

Moderate compatibility. Explores novel biology requiring secondary screening to rule out single-agent redundancies.

⚠ Need a Review Tier (<60%)

Elevated on-target / off-tumor toxicity risk or unfavorable molecular mass ratio. Detailed structural review needed.
